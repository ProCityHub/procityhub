## The Honest Position

Mathematical consistency and software PASS do not establish physical truth. A model that fits synthetic data generated from itself is expected to win — that is a software validation, not an empirical result.

The discriminator's empirical status is **NOT_EVALUATED** until authentic asymmetric-path experimental data are tested. Until then, the hypothesis remains open.

## Engineering vs Physical Claims

The OAB (Observer, Actor, Bridge) and Identity Cylinder constructs in this software are **engineering representations** for organizing prediction → observation → error. They are not physical claims about the double-slit apparatus or about the nature of observation.
